🌐 Quick geography guide
World map and globe

Africa is a continent. It lies in all four hemispheres because the Equator crosses it and the Prime Meridian passes through western Africa. Diagram is schematic.

Oceans and seas

The Atlantic Ocean lies west of Africa and the Indian Ocean lies east. The Mediterranean Sea lies to the north and the Red Sea to the north-east.

Countries, borders and islands

A border marks the boundary between countries. A landlocked country has no coastline. Madagascar is an island country; Zanzibar is part of Tanzania.
